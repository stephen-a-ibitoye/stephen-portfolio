Work process

At every touch-point of the artistic creative process,
we ensure our visual output is resonant, detailed, and impactful.

1. Concept & Sketch

Brainstorming creative themes and initial rough sketches to establish composition, forms, and emotional direction.

2. Linework & Detail

Refining linework, vector geometry, or digital brushwork to create clean contours and expressive strokes.

3. Shading & Color

Applying color palettes, lighting gradients, and realistic/surreal shading to add depth and vibrancy.

4. Final Polish

Adding fine details, textures, highlight effects, and high-resolution export for print and digital publishing.
